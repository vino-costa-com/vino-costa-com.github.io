How it works

The kaizen weekly cycle

A transparent sprint from brief to handoff. Every step is an artifact you can see and sign off on.

MONBrief & moodboard30-min kickoff: goals, audience, metrics, constraints. Palette & direction aligned before pixels.
TUEUX audit & flowsCurrent screens and scenarios reviewed, issues prioritized, hypotheses and flows mapped.
WEDUI concepts2–3 polished concepts of the key screen — you pick the direction.
THUIterate & expandComments folded in; remaining screens drawn on the approved concept.
FRIHandoffDesign system, files and a short report — plus the plan for next week.
Kaizen loop → each week the cycle repeats on the next priority — continuous improvement, 1–2 updates a week, no re-onboarding, pause or resume anytime.
